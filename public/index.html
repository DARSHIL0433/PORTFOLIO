I have extensive expertise with WordPress, WooCommerce, and Shopify, as well as page builders like Elementor. Whether you need a custom WordPress theme, an advanced e-commerce store with secure payment gateways, or a high-converting Shopify store, I develop scalable and reliable solutions.

FAQ 03
WILL MY WEBSITE BE FULLY RESPONSIVE ACROSS ALL DEVICES?
Yes, absolutely. Every project is developed with a mobile-first philosophy, fluid responsive grids, and rigorous cross-device testing. Your website will adapt seamlessly and deliver a fast, frictionless experience on smartphones, tablets, laptops, and ultra-wide desktops.

FAQ 04
CAN I EASILY UPDATE AND MANAGE CONTENT ONCE THE SITE IS LIVE?
Yes. Whether on WordPress, Shopify, or a headless setup, I configure intuitive admin dashboards with customized fields. You and your team can easily update text, swap images, create new pages, or add products without touching code. Full documentation and handoff training are included.

FAQ 05
HOW DO YOU ENSURE FAST LOADING SPEEDS AND SEO OPTIMIZATION?
Speed and SEO are core pillars of my development workflow. I write semantic HTML5 markup, implement lazy loading, optimize images and assets, minimize render-blocking scripts, and follow Core Web Vitals best practices to ensure fast page loads and strong search engine rankings.

Yes! Beyond frontend development, I build end-to-end automated pipelines using Make (Integromat), OpenAI / ChatGPT integrations, and custom webhook connections. This enables automatic lead capture, CRM syncing, customer notifications, and automated workflows that save you hours every week.
